## Introduction
This dashboard helps to check the sales, mainly for 2017, and compare them with the previous year, 2016.

#### Questions to Analyse
1. Compare the sales and profit of 2017 and 2016
2. Compare the sales by Category and Segment of City and Sub-Category
3. Insert a chart to compare sales in each quarter from 2014 to 2017 by sales and price
4. Insert a chart to find the lowest and highest sales by city

### Excel Skills Used:
The following Excel skills were used to analyse 
1. Pivot Table
2. Charts
3. Filters - Slicers
4. Developer - Control Properties -Option Button
5. Developer - Visual Basic (for Refresh Button)
 
### Conclusion
The Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard shows overall positive business performance, with both sales and profit increasing compared with the previous year.

- Sales increased by 20%, reaching approximately $733K for Furniture and $609K for Office Supplies.
- Profit increased by 14%, with Consumer generating the highest profit at approximately $93.4K, followed by Corporate at $81.8K.
- At the sub-category level, Appliances (+65%), Binders (+47%), and Accessories (+43%) show strong sales growth, while Machines (-22%) declined.
- Seattle (+253%) and Columbus (+85%) show particularly strong year-over-year sales growth, while Lafayette (-69%), Detroit (-43%), and Los Angeles (-13%) declined.
- California is the highest-selling state at approximately $457.7K, followed by New York ($310.9K) and Texas ($170.2K).
- Missouri is the lowest-selling state at approximately $22.2K, followed by Rhode Island (22.6K) and Maryland ($23.7K).
- Quarterly sales generally show an upward pattern, with some fluctuations across years and quarters.
 
Overall: 
The dashboard indicates strong year-over-year growth in sales and profit, driven by several high-performing sub-categories, cities, and states. At the same time, declining categories and locations such as Machines, Lafayette, and Detroit had monitored to identify opportunities for improvement.
